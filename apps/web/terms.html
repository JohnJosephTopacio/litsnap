Terms of Use

Last updated: 28 October 2026

1. What LitSnap is

LitSnap is a free, web-based reader for e-books. Files you open are read inside your browser and are never uploaded to us.

2. Your files are your responsibility

Only open files you have the legal right to read. LitSnap doesn't open DRM-protected books and doesn't help remove DRM.

3. Your data stays on your device

Your books, reading positions and settings are stored in your browser. If you clear your browser data, they are deleted, and we can't recover them. LitSnap isn't a backup, so keep your own copies of your books.

4. Lookups and third-party content

When you look up a word, only that word is sent to LitSnap's server, which asks Wiktionary, the Free Dictionary API and Wikipedia for a definition, and Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons for images. Your browser then downloads those images directly from Wikimedia, without cookies, so Wikimedia sees your IP address and which images were shown, under the Wikimedia Foundation's privacy policy.

To find and fix problems, LitSnap's server keeps a record of each lookup for up to 3 days, including the word, but not your IP address or location. Looked-up words and their results are also stored for up to 30 days so that repeat lookups are fast; these aren't linked to you.

Definitions and images belong to their authors and are shown under the licences named next to them. LitSnap doesn't check them and can't promise they're accurate or suitable.
